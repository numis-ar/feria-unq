Cashless payments for your fair

A simple digital wallet for visitors and a clear dashboard for merchants. Visitors top up once, pay any stall by scanning a QR code, and cash out what they do not spend. Try the two flows below.

Try the consumer flow Try the merchant flow

iOS

Step 1

Get the wallet app

Scan the QR code that matches your phone to install the fair wallet. The app is all visitors need to pay at every merchant stall.

Android

Step 2

Top up your wallet

Enter an amount and click the button to generate a QR code. Scan it with the wallet app to load demo money.

Step 3

Pay a merchant

Scan this merchant QR to send a payment from your wallet. The money moves instantly, and both sides see the confirmation.

Step 4

Track orders & payments

Merchants scan this code to open their dashboard and see the latest sales, current balance, and payment history for their stall.
